Beliefs

Where food is, who can be trusted, whose land this is. Learned from what they see and are told, so they can be out of date, or simply mistaken.

Needs

Hunger, energy, health. Food spoils, winter comes, and a body that runs out of reserves dies. Survival is the pressure everything else grows from.

Memory

Who gave them food, who took it, who courted them, who stood by. Kindness and grievance both linger, and shape who they turn to next.

Nothing is scripted

There is no code for markets, chiefs or feuds. There are hungry people, a world with rules, and time. What you see in a run comes out of that.

  • Sharing. Food flows from those with plenty to those without, most readily between kin and friends, and between people who have given before.
  • Ownership. People claim the land around their homes. Owners ward off trespassers; most intruders back off, and a desperate few don't.
  • Conflict. When food runs short and trust runs low, some take by force what they can't find, and those they wrong remember.
  • Families. Courtships turn into bonds, bonds into children. In a big enough world, generations follow one another.
  • Hard years. Droughts and storms come without warning; illness passes between neighbours. Settlements rise, decay and are rebuilt.

Two kinds of mind

The world never decides for anyone. It applies what happens and lets people see it. The choosing is done by one of two minds.
