The world is a grid of land with its own rules. It doesn't care about the people on it, and nothing in it is arranged for them.

## Land

Fertile land comes in **patches**: green valleys and lakeshores, with barren uplands between. The same layout gives the land its **height**: valleys lie low, uplands high. Climbing costs extra energy, and from high ground you see further.

**Food** grows on fertile land and is gathered by hand. **Wood and fiber** grow in stands that regrow slowly, fastest when half grown; an untouched meadow takes about a year to become forest. Thick forest is slow going and hides what lies beyond it. **Stone** lies in the uplands and doesn't come back once quarried.

## Water and wildlife

**Lakes** can't be crossed. Their **fish** breed with the seasons and can be fished down. **Game** lives in herds that breed in spring and summer, wander between neighbouring cells, and drift onto poor land where they don't do well. A hunt is likelier to succeed where herds are thick, and every kill leaves one fewer.

## Seasons and weather

A year is 100 days: spring growth, summer plenty, an autumn that tails off, and a winter in which standing food dies back and almost nothing regrows. On top of the seasons come things no one can plan for: **droughts** that wither growth for weeks, and **storms** that batter shelters and anyone caught outside.

## Bodies

Hunger rises every day, and food carried too long **spoils**; food kept in a storehouse doesn't. Energy runs down with work and comes back with rest. Health falls to starvation, injury and **illness**, a fever that passes between neighbours and leaves survivors immune for a while. People are born, grow up in about fifteen days, can have children until about two thirds of the way through life, and live about four years at most.

## Things people make

From what they gather, people craft **tools** (spears for hunting, axes for wood, poles for fishing) and build **lean-tos, shelters and storehouses**. Buildings take days of work, wear down and eventually collapse unless their owners keep at it.
