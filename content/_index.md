Terrarium is a societal petri dish. You make a world with rules, fill it with AI agents, one mind per person, and see what grows.

It asks one question: **can social structure emerge on its own, from agents free to act within a world's constraints?** Not markets, chiefs or alliances written into the code, but ones that appear because hungry, sociable, mistaken agents found them useful.

It's free and open source. The engine is written in Rust, the viewer runs in a terminal or, compiled to WebAssembly, right here in your browser.
